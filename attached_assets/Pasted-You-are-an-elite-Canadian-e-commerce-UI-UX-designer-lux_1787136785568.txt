→ opens product detail page
→ selects colour/variant where available
→ clicks:

3. Send enquiry notification to business email.
4. Send acknowledgement email to customer.
5. Store product, variant and enquiry source.
6. Show professional success confirmation.

This entire workflow MUST actually work.
